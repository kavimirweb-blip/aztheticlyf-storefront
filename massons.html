Bright windows, pink chairs and tables made for sharing set the tone at Masson’s Defence Colony. Come in for the coffee, settle in for something from the kitchen, and let lunch take its time.

Guests mention the warm service, relaxed atmosphere, pizzas and strong cold coffee. The kind of place that works for a family table, a catch-up or a small pause in the day.
